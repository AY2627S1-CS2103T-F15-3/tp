# UniMate

[![CI Status](https://github.com/AY2627S1-CS2103T-F15-3/tp/workflows/Java%20CI/badge.svg)](https://github.com/AY2627S1-CS2103T-F15-3/tp/actions)

![Ui](docs/images/Ui.png)

**UniMate** is a desktop contact manager for university students who meet
people through modules, group projects, co-curricular activities (CCAs),
orientation programmes, and university events.

It is designed for students with a growing personal contact list who
prefer fast, keyboard-driven interaction.

More often than not, you may remember someone's module, major, year of study, CCA, or the
event where you met them more readily than their full name. UniMate
aims to keep that context alongside contact details, helping you
organise your university contacts and find the right person even
when you cannot remember their name.
